Commutators of the 12 independent As

Only independent generators receive labels A1 through A12 in this table. Each cell is [row A, column A] = row A · column A − column A · row A. Dependent results are combinations of these generators and receive no new number.

Gold cells mark the defining commutators that introduce the nine new independent generators after the initial three. Repeated appearances and the reversed commutators are not new generators.

The original generator scales are retained. Thus factors of π appear in the cells. The diagonal is zero; entries across the diagonal have opposite signs. Scroll horizontally to see all columns. Row and column headers stay visible.
